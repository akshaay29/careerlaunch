# PRD Quality Review — CareerLaunch

## Overall verdict
The PRD is unusually honest about its legal and cost exposure (§8, the Non-Goals, six real Open Questions) and has a disciplined Glossary, unique FR IDs and valid UJ cross-references. What is at risk is coherence and done-ness. The Provider Key (bring-your-own Apify token) decision was folded into the FRs, Glossary and §8 but never carried back into the Vision, UJ-1 or SM-1, so the headline promise and primary journey no longer match the requirements. Meanwhile 15 of 28 FRs have no testable consequence, and the Outreach safety controls (send caps, opt-out, follow-up approval) that §8 leans on as mitigations are the least specified part. It is usable as a risk-aware draft, but not yet ready to hand to epics and stories without a pass on the findings below.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
The big decisions are stated as decisions. The Provider Key cost model is a named Glossary term and has its own FR (FR-28). The shared Listing pool is recorded as accepted (§4.3, OQ-3). The LinkedIn terms-of-service risk is called "high" with mitigations. The Open Questions are genuinely open: Apify actor choice and cost, free-tier host, Google/Microsoft verification. §8 also names what was given up ("needing an Apify account and key adds onboarding friction").

The weakness is that load-bearing premises are written as settled fact while the PRD's own Open Questions say they are not. There are also no `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts anywhere. Several real tensions are left for the reader to discover (see the medium finding below). Three of the six Open Questions (OQ-1, OQ-2, OQ-6) gate the feasibility of the headline features, yet §7.1 simply says "All features in §4, built within 6–10 weeks."

### Findings
- **high** Unverified billing premise is written as fact (§3 Glossary "Provider Key", §4.3 FR-28 consequences, §8 "Cost is shifted to Students") — The Glossary says runs "execute under it, so usage is billed to the Student's Apify account", and FR-28 requires the consent screen to tell Students this. But §10 OQ-1 says "Confirm in Apify's official documentation that runs under a Student's own token bill to that Student", and addendum.md says "per-user billing for a third-party platform is not confirmed in official docs - verify." The whole cost model, three FRs (FR-6, FR-21, FR-28) and the consent copy rest on this, and there is no stated fallback if it is false. *Fix:* Reword the Glossary and FR-28 to be conditional until confirmed. State the decision that applies if it fails (for example, platform-held key with hard caps, or an ATS-API-only Source). Mark OQ-1 as a pre-build gate.
- **high** Feasibility gates are open but the scope says "all features, 6–10 weeks" (§10 OQ-1, OQ-2, OQ-6 vs §7.1) — Google's app verification for sending, and a restricted scope for reply detection, are outside the builder's control and may not fit a 6–10 week window. OQ-6 even floats "reply detection is manual (Student marks replied) at launch", which would change FR-24 and make SM-3 unmeasurable as written. *Fix:* Resolve OQ-6 now as a launch-posture decision. Add an explicit order for the spikes (Apify billing, host quotas, OAuth verification) with a "stop or re-scope" outcome for each.
- **medium** Real tensions are unflagged, and there is no `[NOTE FOR PM]` anywhere — (a) FR-23 requires a Contact who opts out to be "never emailed again by any Student", which means keeping that Contact's address indefinitely, while §5 Privacy says "collect the minimum personal data about Contacts; retain it only as long as needed... 90 days". (b) §1 promises that "the platform does the rest of the legwork" while FR-6 requires every Student to bring their own scraping account. (c) The §4.3 assumption says "the platform does not bear scraping cost", while FR-6 gives Admins an "optional platform-wide cap" and the §11 index says Employee Discovery uses "the platform's provider account". *Fix:* Add callouts at these three points. State that the do-not-contact list is retained as a hash or minimal record with a stated legal basis.

## Substance over theater — adequate
Most of the content is earned. The NFRs carry product-specific thresholds (30,000 active Listings, 0.5 GB quota, alert at 80%, 500 ms search). The single persona (Aarav) drives real decisions: CGPA 7.5, batch 2026, the "Unknown, never Not eligible" rule. The Non-Goals do real work. The Vision and some NFR lines read as furniture.

### Findings
- **medium** Vision could swap into any job-tracker PRD (§1) — "turn a scattered, repetitive job hunt into a short loop" would fit Huntr, Teal or Simplify (all named only in addendum.md). The actual bet, post-apply referral email sent from the Student's own mailbox, is described but never framed as the bet. The Vision also omits the Provider Key model, which is the most distinctive and most constraining product decision. *Fix:* Add two or three sentences. Say what the product believes that the comparables do not, and state the Student-funded scraping model.
- **low** NFR section hygiene (§5) — "Security" and "Secrets" repeat the same encryption requirement. "Reliability" ("idempotent and retry safely") and "Observability" (no retention, no alert path) have no bounds. "Data model: relational, normalised schema" and "external scheduler and job queue" are architecture, which §0 says lives in the addendum. There is no expected Student count anywhere, though it drives Apify cost, scoring volume and the "real use" claim. *Fix:* Move the architecture lines to the addendum, merge the duplicates, and add a target Student count (order of magnitude is enough).

## Strategic coherence — thin
The loop is clear (match, apply, outreach, track) and the FRs follow it. But the PRD reads as a spec for a plan that was revised during discovery without the front matter being revised to match. The Success Metrics measure activity and proxies rather than whether the thesis works.

### Findings
- **critical** Provider Key pivot is missing from Vision, Target User, UJ-1 and SM-1 (§1, §2.3 UJ-1, §9 SM-1 vs FR-6, FR-28) — UJ-1 has Aarav sign up, upload, set Preferences and consent, and then "within the first scheduled run he sees a ranked feed." Under FR-6, ingestion runs only "for each Student with a valid Provider Key", so Aarav must first create an Apify account and paste a token. UJ-1 has no such step, though FR-28 says it "Realizes UJ-1". The no-key path is defined only in one FR bullet: "sees only Listings already in the shared pool". Nothing says who seeds that pool, so the first Student without a key sees an empty feed. SM-1 (70% see a ranked feed within a day) ignores this friction. §8 rates the friction "medium". UX and stories built from UJ-1 would omit the product's biggest onboarding hurdle. *Fix:* Rewrite UJ-1 to include Provider Key setup and its cost disclosure. Add a UJ or edge case for the no-key Student. Say how the pool is seeded at launch. Re-baseline SM-1, and add a metric for key-setup completion. Mention the model in §1 and §2.
- **high** No MVP cut line for 28 FRs and eight capability areas in 6–10 weeks (§7.1, §8 "Solo delivery") — §7.1 is "All features in §4", with build order deferred to epics. The scope includes two mail providers (FR-19 "Gmail or Outlook"), Google sign-in, Employee Discovery, email lookup, reply detection, an Admin console, full-text search and deadline reminders. §8 rates solo delivery "medium" and offers "simple default technology and sequencing in epics" as the mitigation, which is a restatement of the risk, not a mitigation. *Fix:* Tier the FRs (must for the first usable loop, then should, then could). Cut or defer a named set (for example Outlook, FR-12, FR-27 beyond a kill switch). Re-rate the delivery risk.
- **high** Success Metrics test activity, not the thesis (§9) — The thesis is that outreach helps Students stand out, yet there is no interview or referral-to-interview metric. SM-3 (reply rate, 10%) is the nearest proxy and depends on reply detection that OQ-6 says may be manual. SM-2 mixes "weekly active Students" with "40% of signups" without a denominator or cohort. SM-1's time-to-first-feed does not test matching quality. *Fix:* Add one outcome metric (interviews per Application, or a Student-reported "did outreach help"), define SM-3's numerator and denominator (exclude auto-replies and opt-out replies), and state how reply is captured at launch.
- **medium** Build order follows dependency, not the thesis or the risk (§7.1) — Outreach is the differentiator and carries the highest-risk work (OAuth verification, deliverability, do-not-contact, personal-data law), but §7.1 schedules "ingestion, matching and the Tracker first". If Outreach is cut or blocked late, what remains is a weaker clone of existing trackers. *Fix:* Either validate Outreach feasibility early (spike plus thin slice) or say explicitly that v1 could ship as feed plus Tracker only.
- **medium** SM mechanics are loose (§9) — SM-C2 "Counterbalances SM-1 and SM-2", but neither measures alerts (FR-11). SM-1 lists "Validates FR-3, FR-4, FR-5, FR-10" and omits FR-6, FR-9 and FR-28, which a ranked feed requires. SM-4 has no target. SM-C1 and SM-C2 say "keep it low" with no threshold. *Fix:* Link SM-C2 to SM-4 or SM-5, complete SM-1's FR list, and set numeric guardrails.

## Done-ness clarity — thin
Where Consequences exist they are good (FR-1, FR-14 "Unknown, never Not eligible", FR-23 "No email is sent without an Approved state"). But 15 of the 28 FRs have none, including several that carry the core promise. Several FRs rely on adjectives, and a few are under-specified on exactly the behaviours §8 cites as safety mitigations.

### Findings
- **high** 15 of 28 FRs have no testable consequence (§4) — FR-5, FR-7, FR-10, FR-11, FR-12, FR-15, FR-16, FR-17, FR-18, FR-20, FR-22, FR-24, FR-25, FR-26 and FR-27 carry no Consequences block. Examples: FR-10 ("ranked by Match Score and filtered by their Preferences" has no ordering or tie rule, no pagination, no behaviour for a below-threshold Listing). FR-12 ("lower-scoring" and "most often" are undefined, with no top-N). FR-20 ("ranked by relevance (team, seniority, alumni or existing connection)" has no weighting and no number of Contacts proposed). FR-22 ("personalised" has no checkable condition, for example that it cites a Listing or Resume fact). FR-24 (reply, opt-out, Rejected and Withdrawn stops are listed but not given as testable bullets). FR-27 ("review reports of misuse"). *Fix:* Add one to three bullets per FR, each with a verifiable condition or number.
- **high** Send caps and opt-out are named but have no values or mechanism (§4.7 FR-23, §8 "Anti-spam") — "Daily and per-company send caps apply" and UJ-3's "within his daily send cap" give no number. The opt-out line has no stated mechanism: emails are sent from the Student's own Gmail with, per the FR-19 assumption, "no shared sending domain". A link needs a platform-hosted endpoint, and a reply-based opt-out needs reply parsing, which OQ-6 says may not be available. How a Contact lands on the do-not-contact list is therefore undefined. FR-27 lets Admins "review reports of misuse", but no FR lets anyone file one. *Fix:* Set default caps (for example N per day, M per company per week), define the opt-out mechanism, and add the intake path for abuse reports.
- **high** Follow-ups contradict the approval rule and have no rules of their own (§3 "Follow-up", FR-23 vs FR-24, UJ-3) — The Glossary defines a Follow-up as "a scheduled repeat Outreach". FR-23 says "No email is sent without an Approved state", but FR-24 and UJ-3 ("schedules a follow-up in 5 days") have Follow-ups sent without a stated approval step. Nothing sets the number of Follow-ups, the interval (5 days appears only in UJ-3) or whether the Student can edit the text. It also does not stop at Offer. *Fix:* State whether the approval of an Outreach covers its scheduled Follow-ups or each needs its own. Cap the count and set the interval. Add a consequence for each stop condition.
- **high** Eligibility and deadlines depend on data that ingestion does not capture (§4.3 FR-6, §4.5 FR-14 and FR-15, UJ-2) — FR-6 stores "title, company, location, description, posted date, deadline when present, and source URL". There are no fields for batch year, minimum CGPA, degree or work authorisation. No FR extracts these from descriptions, and most LinkedIn postings state none. FR-14 will therefore mostly return "Unknown", and FR-15 reminders only apply to Listings that carry a deadline. UJ-2's climax ("platform marks it Eligible") may rarely occur. *Fix:* Add an FR for extracting criteria and deadline from the description, with a stated confidence and an "unknown" rate target. Or reduce UJ-2 and FR-14/FR-15 to what the Source can actually provide.
- **medium** Vague or unmeasurable phrases (various) — "clear message" (FR-3, FR-28), "securely" (FR-26), "where possible" and the undefined "confidence level" with no threshold for "low-confidence" (FR-21), "typo tolerance" (FR-13), "a set number of days" with no default or range (FR-15), "retried with backoff" with no count or schedule (FR-6), "Typical searches return in under 500 ms" with no percentile (FR-13 and §5), and "feed pages load in under 2 s" with no conditions (§5). *Fix:* Replace each with a bound (p95 under stated load, N retries, enumerated confidence levels, exact error strings or codes).
- **medium** Spend-cap semantics and automatic spend are unspecified (FR-6, FR-20, FR-21, FR-28, UJ-3) — The PRD does not say whether caps are enforced on the Apify side or are a platform estimate, what the defaults are, or what happens to a run in flight. FR-21 stops lookups at the cap, but FR-20 (Employee Discovery) has no such bullet. In UJ-3, marking an Application "Applied" triggers discovery and lookup automatically, so it spends the Student's money without an estimate or confirmation, though FR-28 promises "estimated and actual usage per run". *Fix:* Define cap units, defaults and enforcement. Apply the cap to FR-20. State whether discovery needs an opt-in or a cost preview.
- **medium** Ingestion cadence and scoring dynamics are undefined (FR-3, FR-4, FR-6, UJ-1, UJ-4) — FR-6 says "scheduled collection jobs" with no frequency, though UJ-1's "within the first scheduled run" and SM-1's "within a day" depend on it. FR-3 re-scores "the Student's Listings" on every active-Resume change with no time bound, over a pool of up to 30,000. FR-4's "Student edits persist across Resume re-uploads unless explicitly reset" conflicts with UJ-4's improved re-upload, because no merge rule says whether a stale correction beats a newly extracted skill. *Fix:* Set the schedule, a re-score completion target, and a merge rule for edits versus extraction.
- **medium** "No shared sending domain" conflicts with platform email (§4.7 FR-19 assumption vs FR-11, FR-15) — FR-11 sends alerts "by email or in-app" and FR-15 sends reminders, and sign-in and password flows need email too. Those must come from some platform sender. The assumption as worded says the platform has none. *Fix:* Scope the assumption to Outreach only, and state the sender for platform notifications.
- **low** Data lifecycle edges (FR-2, FR-8, §5 Storage, addendum.md) — FR-8 marks a Listing expired when it is "no longer found at the Source", but with a Preferences-scoped, per-Student pool, absence from one Student's run does not mean it was removed. Eviction orders by "oldest... and the lowest relevance to current Preferences" without saying whose Preferences. addendum.md puts 20–30k Listings at 150–400 MB plus about 45 MB of embeddings, which already sits near the 0.5 GB quota before Students, Applications, Outreach, Contacts, logs and queue tables. FR-2's deletion bullet omits Skill Profile, Preferences, Provider Key, Contacts and documents, though the description says "all data". *Fix:* Define expiry as absence across N consecutive runs of the same query. Order eviction as one stated rule. Budget the non-Listing tables. Complete the FR-2 deletion list.

## Scope honesty — adequate
§6 Non-Goals and §7.2 are explicit and do real work, including "No auto-applying", "No logging in as the Student on LinkedIn", and "No mass or bulk email blasts". Inferences are tagged inline (18 `[ASSUMPTION]` tags) and indexed. Open-items density is 18 assumptions plus 6 Open Questions plus 0 `[NOTE FOR PM]`. For a PRD about to feed epics, with legal exposure and third-party personal data, that is high and several of the items are blocking, not routine. Omissions that sit in the Glossary or Addendum, rather than in the Non-Goals, are where scope is silently assumed.

### Findings
- **medium** Capabilities in the Glossary without FRs, and attributes dropped (§3, §4.2 FR-5, §4.8 FR-27, UJ-4) — The Glossary's Eligibility lists "work authorisation", but FR-5 captures only "batch year, CGPA, degree". The Admin definition includes "abuse reports" and FR-27 reviews them, with no FR to file one. UJ-4 lists Status without "Withdrawn", which the Glossary and FR-24 include. *Fix:* Either add the attribute and the report intake, or list them as `[NON-GOAL for MVP]`.
- **medium** No decision on alternative Sources despite a "high" LinkedIn risk (§4.3, §8, addendum.md) — The addendum lists Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Adzuna and RemoteOK as lower-risk Sources. The PRD mentions only that a Source Adapter allows "another Source" later and that manual entry (FR-18) is the only fallback. If LinkedIn access is lost "without notice", the feed is empty. *Fix:* State whether one ATS or API Source is in v1 or an explicit non-goal, with the reason.

## Downstream usability — adequate
This PRD is stated to feed UX, architecture and stories, so the dimension matters. A Glossary exists and is mostly respected. FR IDs 1–28 are unique and contiguous (though FR-28 sits out of order in §4.3), and all FR, UJ and SM references resolve except one section pointer (see Mechanical notes). All four UJs name a protagonist with inline context. The weaker points are traceability in the FR-to-UJ direction and a few Glossary overlaps.

### Findings
- **medium** FR-to-UJ traceability is incomplete (§4, §2.3) — UJ-3 needs FR-19 (send from Gmail), FR-21 (look up work emails) and FR-25 (LinkedIn message draft fallback), and none of them carries "Realizes UJ-3". FR-2, FR-6, FR-7, FR-8, FR-18 and FR-27 have no UJ mapping, and UJ-1 lacks the Provider Key step that FR-28 claims to realize. Story creation that sources from "Realizes" will drop these. *Fix:* Add Realizes tags, and rewrite UJ-1 per the critical finding above.
- **medium** Glossary terms overlap or are undefined (§3, passim) — (1) **Application** is "a Student's record of applying to a Listing", yet "Saved" is an Application Status (§3, UJ-3, FR-16) and FR-15 "save a Listing" overlaps it, so a Listing that has not been applied to is still an Application. (2) **Resume** vs "tailored Resume" vs documents: UJ-4 and FR-16 treat a tailored Resume as a Resume version, while FR-26 files "tailored Resumes" under "Student-uploaded documents". (3) "provider" and "Source or provider" (FR-7, FR-27) are used as a concept with no Glossary entry, apart from "Provider Key". (4) "minimum Match Score 70" (UJ-1) vs "Match Score threshold" (§3, FR-5, FR-11). (5) "the user has accepted the terms-of-service risk" (§4.3, §10 OQ-3, §11) uses "user" for the product owner, while elsewhere the end-user is a Student. The risk acceptance is also unnamed and undated. *Fix:* Define Application as including Saved, add a Provider entry, unify "threshold", and replace "the user" with the named product owner and a date.

## Shape fit — adequate
This is a consumer product with three stakeholder types: Students, Admins, and third-party Contacts who receive the emails. UJs with named protagonists are load-bearing, and they are present. The PRD is also compliance-adjacent (GDPR, DPDP, CAN-SPAM, LinkedIn terms), so constraint traceability matters. For a solo build the rigor is appropriate, but it is unevenly distributed.

### Findings
- **medium** Precision is spent on hosting economics, not on product behaviour (§4.3 FR-8, §5 vs §4.7 FR-23, FR-24) — The PRD fixes "about 30,000 active Listings", "4,000 characters", "30 days", "about 0.5 GB" and "an alert at 80%", while the behaviours that determine whether real people are harmed or helped (send caps, follow-up cadence and approval, opt-out mechanism, match-quality bar) are unspecified. *Fix:* Rebalance. Trim infra detail to the addendum and spend that space on Outreach and matching acceptance criteria.
- **medium** UJ set is one persona on the happy path, and compliance constraints are not traced to FRs (§2.3, §5, §8) — All four UJs are Aarav and succeed. There is no UJ for a Contact who opts out or receives a mistaken email, for the Admin, or for a Student without a Provider Key, and those are the flows where the risk lives. §8 mitigations cite FR IDs only for FR-6, FR-7, FR-18 and FR-28. Retention limits, global do-not-contact and stop-on-reply are named without FR IDs, and the 90-day Contact retention exists only as an `[ASSUMPTION]` in §5, not as an FR that purges data. *Fix:* Add one Contact or opt-out UJ and one no-key Student UJ. Add FR IDs next to each §8 mitigation, and add an FR for the Contact-data purge.
